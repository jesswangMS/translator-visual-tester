How to use:

  1. Click "Start Session" and allow microphone access
  2. Speak clearly in either English or Chinese
  3. The translator will automatically detect your language and translate to the other
  4. The avatar will listen, think, and speak the translation
  5. After translation, the avatar returns to listening automatically
  6. Click "Stop Session" when finished

Note: Works best in Chrome or Edge browsers
